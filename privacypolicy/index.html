PRIVACY POLICY.

Effective date: October 25, 2026. This policy describes how Nancy Games handles information through this website.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Nancy Games website at nancygames.space. Individual Nancy Games mobile applications may have separate privacy policies that describe their specific data practices, including any third-party services they use.

2. Information You Provide

If you contact us by email, we may receive your email address, name (if provided), and the contents of your message. We use this information to respond to your inquiry and provide support.

3. Website Hosting and Technical Information

This website may be hosted using GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical data such as IP addresses and request information for security, delivery, and operational purposes under its own policies. This website does not intentionally use advertising or analytics tracking scripts.

4. How We Use Information

We use information received through email to answer questions, address support requests, communicate about inquiries, and maintain the security of our communications.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal information submitted through our support email. Information may be processed by our email and hosting service providers or disclosed where legally required.

6. Data Retention

We retain support correspondence only for as long as reasonably necessary for responding to requests, maintaining records, or meeting applicable legal obligations.

7. Your Choices

You may contact us to request information about personal data you have provided through our website or to request correction or deletion, subject to applicable law.

8. Children's Privacy

This website is intended to provide information about our studio and is not designed to collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us.

9. Updates

We may update this policy when our website or practices change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, email support@nancygames.space.

Important: This website policy is a starter template. Review it against your actual website practices before publishing. It does not automatically cover data collection inside your mobile games.
